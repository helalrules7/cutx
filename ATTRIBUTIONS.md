# Attributions

## Sounds

CutX ships seven short sounds in `Resources/sounds/`. All of them are regenerated
by `scripts/make-sounds.py`.

### Synthesized

`snip.wav`, `tick.wav`, and `paste.wav` are generated from scratch by
`scripts/make-sounds.py`. They are part of this project and covered by its MIT
license.

### Foley, from Pixabay

`scissors.wav`, `paper.wav`, `knife.wav`, and `bush.wav` are trimmed to 130 ms
from royalty-free sound effects downloaded from
[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/cut/). The untouched originals
are kept in `assets/sounds-original/` so the trim stays reproducible.

| File | Original | Author | Source |

| `paper.wav` | Tear a Paper | creatorshome | https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/tear-a-paper-328149/ |
| `knife.wav` | Knife Cut Veggies Foley 2 | floraphonic | https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/knife-cut-veggies-foley-2-211702/ |
